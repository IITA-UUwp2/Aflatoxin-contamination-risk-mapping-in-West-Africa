## Soybean Rust Collection Points

### Background

**Soybean rust** is a fungal disease that affects soybean plants and can cause significant reductions in crop yield, particularly under favourable environmental conditions. The disease is caused mainly by *Phakopsora pachyrhizi*, commonly known as Asian soybean rust. The disease spreads through airborne spores and can infect soybean crops across large geographical areas.

### Mapping of Soybean Rust Collection Points

For this project, **two maps were prepared to visualise and compare soybean rust collection points** across Africa.

The first map focuses on **East and Southern Africa**, providing a more detailed view of the spatial distribution of the sampling points within these regions. The second map covers the **whole of Africa**, providing a broader continental perspective of the distribution of the soybean rust collection points.

Each map contains two datasets:

- **Old collection points:** These are the points that were previously shared with me by **Dr. Francis Muthoni**.
- **New collection points:** These are the points that were downloaded from **Figshare**.

The two datasets were then **overlaid on the same maps** to facilitate a direct spatial comparison between the previously available collection points and the newly obtained points. This comparison helps to identify areas where the datasets overlap, as well as locations represented by the new dataset that may not have been included in the original collection points.
