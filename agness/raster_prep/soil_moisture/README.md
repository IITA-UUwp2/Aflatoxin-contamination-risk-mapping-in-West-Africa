Monthly and seasonal-aggregate volumetric soil moisture GeoTIFFs from
ERA5-Land, per agro-ecological zone, for sample years 2012, 2013,
2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, and 2024. Includes both the top soil layer
(0-7cm) and the second layer (7-28cm). Seasonal bands
are the mean across the growing season (soil moisture is a state,
not an accumulating quantity). Units: m³/m³ (volumetric water
content), no conversion applied.
